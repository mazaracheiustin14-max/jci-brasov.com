JCI Brașov Beyond the Initiative

The evening that marks JCI Brașov's move from initiative group to full member chapter of JCI Romania. Two panels, eight speakers from business, education and public administration, and two Blitz Networking sessions designed for real connections and lasting partnerships.

DateThursday, 26 November
Time5:00 pm – 10:30 pm
VenueCATTIA Brașov
AddressStr. Institutului 35
EntryFree, registration required
Attendeesaround 150
