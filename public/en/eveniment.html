JCI Brașov Beyond the Initiative

The event took place on Thursday, 26 November 2026. Thank you to our speakers, our partners and everyone who joined us. The programme, speakers and partners of the evening remain below.

The evening that marks JCI Brașov's move from initiative group to full member chapter of JCI Romania. Two panels, seven speakers from business, education and public administration, and two Blitz Networking sessions designed for real connections and lasting partnerships.

Moderator

Event moderatorVeronica CostacheEntrepreneur, President of the OFA Brașov Employers' Association

An entrepreneur with more than 20 years of experience in communication and in developing educational and training projects, focused on personal development, life skills and careers for young people.

Read more

In London she founded and runs non-formal education and personal development projects, and took part in the government programme Help to Grow, supporting entrepreneurs and company employees through coaching, personal development and wellbeing programmes centred on building self-leadership.

In Brașov she runs, together with her family, an educational centre that develops musical skills in children and young people, combining arts education with the ability to manage emotions, stress and one's own development path.

She is President of the OFA Brașov Employers' Association (Organisation of Women Entrepreneurs) and is actively involved in projects on entrepreneurship, leadership and professional development.

Her work rests on a simple idea: lasting professional growth starts with the ability to lead yourself, through clarity, responsibility, adaptability and confidence.
