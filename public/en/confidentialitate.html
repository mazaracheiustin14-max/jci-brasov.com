Privacy

Privacy and cookie policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This page explains which personal data JCI Brașov collects through this website, why we use it, how long we keep it and what rights you have. We collect only what our activity requires and we never sell data.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is JCI Brașov, the Brașov local organisation of the Federation “Junior Chamber International România”, part of the Junior Chamber International network. For any question or request about your data, write to contact@jci-brasov.com or call +40 790 827 426.

You may also opt in to invitations to future JCI Brașov events, or to being contacted by BCR Asigurări, the event partner. In the latter case we share only your name, email and phone number with BCR Asigurări.

3. Who receives the data

We use providers that process data on our behalf, under contract, solely for the services below:

  • Cloudflare: website hosting, the registration database, email forwarding, the “Ask us” assistant and visit statistics;
  • Google (Gmail): correspondence and sending personal invitations;
  • FormSubmit: forwarding form messages to our inbox;
  • Stripe: processing VIP ticket payments;
  • Meta Platforms Ireland: Meta Pixel, only with your consent; for this collection JCI Brașov and Meta act as joint controllers;
  • BCR Asigurări: only if you ticked the specific consent described above;
  • the JCI România Federation: as needed for membership and sponsorship contracts.

Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Such transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or on standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

4. How long we keep it

  • event registrations: up to 12 months after the event, then deleted; if you asked for future invitations, until you withdraw consent;
  • membership applications: for the duration of membership; unfinished applications, up to 12 months;
  • sponsorship requests: for the duration of the partnership and up to 3 years after the last correspondence, except documents we must keep by law;
  • payment data: for the period required by accounting law;
  • questions sent to the assistant: 90 days;
  • technical security identifiers: 2 days.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict processing, to data portability and to object, as well as the right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior processing. To exercise them, write to contact@jci-brasov.com; we reply within one month. If you believe your rights have been infringed, you may lodge a complaint with the Romanian data protection authority, ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro).

7. Security and age

The website uses encrypted connections, strict security policies and limits on automated submissions; only members of the organising team can access registration data. JCI is open to people aged 18 to 40; we do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Changes

We update this policy whenever our services or the way we process data change. The date of the latest update appears at the top of this page.

JCI Brașov is part of JCI Romania and Junior Chamber International, the global network of young leaders aged 18 to 40.
