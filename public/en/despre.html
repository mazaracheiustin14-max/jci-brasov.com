About JCI Brașov

A global movement of young leaders, rooted in Brașov since 2003.

Not just another network. A movement.

JCI is a global network of young leaders who learn by doing, grow by sharing and lead by acting.

The world needs people who don't wait for change, but make it happen. Turning that drive into real impact takes opportunity, fresh perspective and a community that believes in your potential.

At JCI Brașov, members take ownership of projects in business, culture and the community, and push each other to raise the bar. JCI was founded in 1915 and has been active in Romania since 2003. Brașov was one of the cities that founded the national federation.

JCI Mission

We develop young leaders and empower them to change the world for the better

We are building the leading global network of young leaders

History

A city with a JCI track record

Brașov has given JCI a national president who went on to become world vice president, as well as two JCI Senators. Today's generation is building on that legacy.

  1. 1915

    Where it all began

    JCI is founded in St. Louis, USA. Today it is active in more than 100 countries.

  2. 1994

    A first attempt in Romania

    A group of pioneers, including Ilona Görög, sets out to bring JCI to Romania. The effort does not succeed at the time, but it lays the groundwork for what follows.

  3. 2003

    JCI Romania is born

    JCI Bucharest, JCI Timișoara, JCI Constanța and JCI Brașov form the JCI Romania federation, affiliated with the world organisation.

  4. 2010

    JCI Brașov relaunches

    The local chapter is relaunched around four priorities: entrepreneurship, individual development, international development and community.

  5. 2011–2012

    Years of growth

    Ionuț Țața leads JCI Brașov in 2011, followed by Sorin Peligrad in 2012.

  6. 2013–2014

    Brașov on the national and world stage

    Ionuț Țața becomes President of JCI Romania in 2013 and JCI World Vice President in 2014. In 2014, Alin Grigoraș is President of JCI Brașov.

  7. 2026

    A new generation

    Backed by Sorin Peligrad and JCI Romania, the initiative group becomes a full member chapter. We celebrate the milestone on 26 November at CATTIA.

JCI Brașov is part of JCI Romania and Junior Chamber International, the global network of young leaders aged 18 to 40.
