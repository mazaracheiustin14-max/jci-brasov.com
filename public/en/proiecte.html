What we're building

Projects that start from real community needs, linked to the Sustainable Development Goals.

Every JCI project starts from a real community need and is linked to the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

JCI Romania national project, delivered in Brașov

We collect toys that can be reused and refurbish them, and the results of the campaign reach children in oncology wards and families in need.

JCI Brașov podcast, in preparation

Conversations with the people shaping the city: entrepreneurs, local leaders and people whose stories are worth hearing. Seasons of 16 episodes, filmed in a studio.

2027 Projects

We are building our 2027 project portfolio around three areas: education, entrepreneurship and community development. The full calendar will be announced shortly.
