Become a member

Aged 18 to 40 and ready to make a difference?

Students, entrepreneurs, employees, managers, freelancers or consultants: at JCI, what matters is what you want to build, not where you come from.

  1. Join the community

    Our WhatsApp group is where we announce events and opportunities.

  2. Come to an event

    Meet the people and see how we work. The first step is 26 November.

  3. Pick a project

    Join a team and learn by doing, with support from experienced members.

  4. Become a member

    Get access to training, national competitions and the international JCI network.

  • Courses and training
  • International opportunities
  • Community impact
  • Recognition and awards
  • Personal growth
