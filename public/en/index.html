About us

Not just another network. A movement.

JCI is a global network of young leaders who learn by doing, grow by sharing and lead by acting. Brașov has been part of it since 2003.

  • 01

    Personal growth

    Training, public speaking and a new leadership role every year.

  • 02

    Business

    Entrepreneurship, networking and collaboration between members and partners.

  • 03

    Community

    Local projects with measurable impact, aligned with the Sustainable Development Goals.

  • 04

    International

    Conferences, exchanges and connections in more than 100 countries.

Become a member

Aged 18 to 40 and ready to make a difference?

Students, entrepreneurs, employees, managers, freelancers or consultants: at JCI, what matters is what you want to build, not where you come from.

  1. Join the community

    Our WhatsApp group is where we announce events and opportunities.

  2. Come to an event

    Meet the people and see how we work. The first step is 26 November.

  3. Pick a project

    Join a team and learn by doing, with support from experienced members.

  4. Become a member

    Get access to training, national competitions and the international JCI network.
